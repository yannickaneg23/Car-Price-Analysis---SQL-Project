Executive Summary: Used-Car Pricing & Market Valuation AnalysisProject Title: Car Price Analysis — SQL Portfolio ProjectAuthor: Yannick Hassan | Data AnalystDatabase System: MySQL 8.0Dataset Scope: 6,643 Indian Used Vehicle Listings (1983–2020)1. Project Background & Business ContextThe secondary automotive market presents complex pricing dynamics influenced by vehicle age, accumulated mileage, powertrain options, and ownership history. This project analyzed 6,643 used car listings to evaluate key price drivers, identify depreciation patterns, and discover value opportunities.By employing MySQL 8.0 for regex-based text parsing, schema modification, conditional aggregations, and CTE-driven ratio analyses, this study provides data-backed recommendations for inventory managers, pricing analysts, and individual sellers.2. Core Analytical FindingsDimension / VariableAnalytical FindingBusiness ImplicationTransmission TypeAutomatic vehicles average ₹1,322,984 vs. ₹455,306 for manual vehicles (~3x premium).Automatics represent luxury/upper-segment inventory and warrant dedicated financing packages.Seller ChannelDealer listings average ₹935,000, while private seller listings average ₹484,000 (~2x gap).Dealer markups reflect reconditioning and trust; private sellers lose equity without third-party validation.Powertrain / FuelDiesel models average ₹648,567 vs. ₹387,977 for Petrol models (+67% premium).Diesel engines maintain capital value far longer over time due to buyer demand for efficiency.Ownership TierEach additional owner reduces resale valuation by ₹100,000 – ₹200,000.Depreciation steepens past 1st ownership; multi-owner cars require faster inventory turn times.3. Key Strategic RecommendationsHigh PriorityTargeted Financing for Automatics: Develop specialized financing and extended warranty bundles for automatic inventory to capture buyer willingness to pay in the ₹1.3M+ pricing tier.Third-Party Certification for Private Sellers: Offer a paid "Vehicle Condition & Inspection Certificate" product for individual sellers to help bridge the ₹450K price gap against dealer listings.Medium PriorityOptimized Procurement for Used Diesel Inventory: Prioritize second-hand diesel vehicles with verified service histories, pricing them confidently above petrol equivalents even as vehicle age increases.Low PriorityDynamic Trade-In Valuation Rules: Adjust trade-in pricing algorithms to apply non-linear valuation markdowns as soon as a vehicle transitions to second- or third-owner status.4.
